# Learnova AI — Stakeholder Analysis

**Status:** Day 1 draft

| Stakeholder | Interest / role | Engagement needed |
|---|---|---|
| Student project owner | Defines goals, builds and evaluates the system | Confirm deadline, scope, budget, and academic requirements |
| University students | Primary users; upload materials and use study workflows | Short interviews or usability review; validate pain points and priorities |
| Academic supervisor / assessor | Reviews academic value, method, and deliverables | Confirm project expectations, evaluation criteria, and acceptable use of AI |
| Course instructors (optional) | May advise on course-aligned practice and feedback | Consult only if relevant and available; system does not replace their judgment |
| AI, hosting, storage, and search providers | Process data and deliver infrastructure | Review current terms, privacy controls, limits, costs, and deletion mechanisms |
| Project evaluator / portfolio reviewer | Assesses quality and demonstrable outcomes | Provide clear documentation, reproducible demo flow, and limitations |

## User roles (initial)

- **Student:** owns an account, documents, conversations, and study activity.
- **Project administrator:** limited operational role for managing the demonstrator; access to student content should not be assumed or granted by default.

Detailed permissions belong in Day 2 requirements and security design.

## Engagement plan

Before fixing the MVP, gather feedback from a small set of representative university students and the academic supervisor. Ask about real workflows, file types, trust, privacy, and the single most valuable first feature. Do not collect unnecessary personal or academic data during research.
